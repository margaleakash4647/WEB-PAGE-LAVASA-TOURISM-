About Lavasa

A City Born
from Nature's Lap

Lavasa is a privately planned hill city in Maharashtra, built on the shores of Warasgaon Lake against the dramatic backdrop of the Sahyadri mountain range. Architecturally inspired by the Italian town of Portofino, its colourful waterfront buildings, cobblestone promenades, and terraced streets create a uniquely European atmosphere in the heart of the Western Ghats.

Sprawling across 25,000 acres and designed by renowned architects from HOK, USA, Lavasa is a popular weekend destination from Pune and Mumbai. With lush greenery covering 70% of the area, it blends urban comfort with natural serenity.

"Where the hills whisper, the lakes shimmer, and the city breathes in colour."

Must-Visit Destinations

Top Places to Explore

01
Lakeside Promenade
Lavasa's iconic waterfront stretch — colourful Italian-inspired buildings, open-air cafés, souvenir stalls, and pedal boating on Dasve Lake. Perfect for a sunset stroll with the hills glowing in the background.
⭐ Most Popular
02
Dasve Dam
A massive dam on the Muthu River offering sweeping reservoir views. Especially dramatic in monsoon when the overflow spills over, and at golden hour when the hills turn orange.
🌅 Scenic Viewpoint
03
Dasve Lake & Point
Calm lake waters ringed by green hills. Dasve Point is perfect for birdwatching — migratory species flock here — and for quiet picnics with views of Lavasa's colourful skyline reflected in the water.
🐦 Nature Spot
04
Nature Trail
A well-marked walking trail through lush Sahyadri forests, past cascading waterfalls and serene river stretches, with a dedicated butterfly stop. A paradise for photographers and nature lovers.
🌿 Trekking
05
Bamboosa Cultural Centre
An eco-tourism venture showcasing bamboo crafts and sustainable living. Explore the bamboo nursery, watch village artisans at work, and shop eco-friendly products while supporting local livelihoods.
🎋 Cultural Experience
06
Tikona Fort
A moderate trek to this ancient Sahyadri hill fort rewards with sweeping panoramic views of the Western Ghats. History buffs will love exploring the ruined battlements and garrison walls.
🏰 Heritage Trek

Things To Do

Adventure & Experiences

🚣
Water Sports

Lakeshore Water Sports at Town Hall offers kayaking, jet skiing, bumper boats, pedal boats, and water volleyball on Dasve Lake — perfect for families and thrill-seekers alike.

🚴
Cycling

Cycle the scenic lakeside promenade or take on winding hill roads at your own pace. Rentals are available near Town Hall for a leisurely spin with magnificent valley views.

🧖
Dasvino Spa

Sprawling across 1.03 hectares, Dasvino Country Club offers rejuvenating spa therapies, gymnasium, indoor pool, squash court, and multi-cuisine dining in lush surroundings.

🌊
Devkund Waterfall

A magnificent 220-ft waterfall near Bhira. Trek through Sahyadri forests to this hidden gem — camping, nature trails, and village home-cooked food make it an unforgettable side trip.

Plan Your Trip

Best Time to Visit Lavasa

🌧️
Monsoon
June – September

Lavasa at its lush dramatic best. Misty Sahyadri hills, gushing waterfalls and cool breezes. Slightly slippery roads but stunning views.

🌤️
Post-Monsoon
October – February

Pleasant weather, crystal-clear skies, and emerald green landscapes. Best for outdoor activities, trekking, and photography.

☀️
Summer
March – May

Warmer but manageable at altitude. Fewer crowds mean better deals and peaceful exploration of the lakeside and trails.

How to Reach

Plan Your
Journey

✈️
By Air

The nearest airport is Pune International Airport, approximately 58 km away. Taxis and app-based cabs are readily available for the scenic 1.5–2 hour drive through Mulshi ghat roads.

🚆
By Train

Pune Railway Station is the nearest railhead (~60 km). From there, hire a cab or take a shared vehicle via the NH 48 and Paud–Mulshi road route.

🚗
By Road

From Pune ~65 km (~1.5 hrs) via Katraj–Pirangut. From Mumbai ~185 km (~4 hrs) via Pune Expressway. The ghat drive through the hills is scenic and worthwhile.

🏨
Where to Stay

Options include The Waterfront Shaw, Fortune Select Dasve, Ekaant The Retreat, and serviced apartments for extended stays. Book well in advance on weekends and during monsoon.

🍽️
Where to Eat

Popular spots: The Deck (lake views), Chor Bizarre, All American Diner, JenJon's Brewberry Café, and Orient 8. The promenade also has street food stalls and bakeries.
